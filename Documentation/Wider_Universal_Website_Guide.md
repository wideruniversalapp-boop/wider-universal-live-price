# Part 1: What we have built

We have built our own **online product catalog and price list** for Wider Universal. It has two sides.

**1. The public website** (anyone can open it):
- About **1,291 products** from 11 brands, including Dahua, Ajax, Imou, YLI, Fyber, ITC, Legrand, NJOY and STARLINK.
- Prices in **Saudi Riyals (SAR)**. These are Wider Universal's own selling prices.
- Search, brand filters and category filters.
- A **Quote list**: customers pick products and quantities, then send the list to one of our branches by WhatsApp or email.
- A **downloadable PDF price list** carrying our logo and contact details.
- A **Contact page** with our three branches.
- Green **price-drop tags** when we lower a price.
- It works on phones, tablets, laptops and large monitors.

**2. The admin area** (only for authorised staff):
- Change prices, offers and stock.
- Add, edit or hide products.
- Update many products at once from an Excel spreadsheet. A whole import can be undone.
- Create PDF price lists.
- Edit the logo, website texts, header, footer, contacts and page layout.
- See a full history of every price change and settings change, with who made it and when.

Changes made in the admin area appear on the website **straight away**. No programmer is needed for everyday work.

### Main features at a glance

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Product catalog | All products grouped by category, with search and filters |
| Brand spotlight | A banner at the top changes to match the brand being viewed |
| Product details | Click any product to see its picture, specifications, datasheet and price |

| Price-drop tags | Shows "PRICE ↓ 8%" for 30 days after we lower a price |
| Quote list | Customers build a list and send it to a branch |

| Admin dashboard | Manage everything without touching code |
| History | Every price and settings change is recorded and cannot be deleted |

# Part 2: Using the public website

## 2.1 The home page (catalog)

The home page is the catalog. From top to bottom:

1. **Header:** our logo, the menu (Catalog, Offers, Contact, **Quote list**) and a **Main Website** button. The Main Website button opens wideruniversal.com in a new tab.
2. **Brand spotlight:** a large banner with a title and pictures. It also shows "Live price list · updated *date*", the date prices were last updated.
3. **Search box:** type a model number, product code or name, for example `IPC-HDW2841` or `intercom`.
4. **Brand buttons ("chips"):** click a brand to see only its products. Each button shows how many products that brand has. There are also **Offers** and **Price drops** buttons.
5. **Category, Sort and In stock filters**, to narrow the list further. Click **Reset** to clear all filters.
6. **Product list:** products grouped by category. Each category shows 8 products at first; click **Show all** to see the rest.

## 2.2 Looking at a product

- **Click any product row.** It opens in place and shows:
  - a larger picture;
  - key specifications;
  - a datasheet link, when one is available;
  - the price, a quantity box, **Add to quote list** and **Enquire on WhatsApp**.
- To see everything about a product on its own page, click the link to the full product page. You can share that page's address with a customer.

### What the labels mean

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SAR 1,250** | Our selling price |
| Price shown with an older price crossed out, plus an **OFFER** label | A special offer is running |
| Green **PRICE ↓ 8%** tag | We lowered this price within the last 30 days. The old price is shown crossed out. |
| **Price on request** | No price is published. The customer should contact us. |
| **In stock / Low stock / Out of stock / On request** | Availability. Admin can hide this if wanted. |

## 2.3 The quote list (for customers)

The quote list works like a shopping basket, but no payment is taken. It is a **request for a quotation**.

1. Open a product and choose a quantity.
2. Click **Add to quote list**. A small counter appears next to **Quote list** in the header.
3. When finished, click **Quote list** in the header, or the bar at the bottom of the screen.
4. On the quote page you can:
   - change quantities or remove items;
   - see the total. Items without a price are marked "on request";
   - **choose a branch and a contact person**;
   - optionally add your name, company, phone and notes.
5. Send the request using one of these buttons:
   - **Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the full list ready to send to that salesperson.
   - **Send by email** opens the email app with the list filled in.
   - **Copy request text** copies the list so it can be pasted anywhere.

> **Good to know:** the quote list is saved only on the customer's own phone or computer. Nothing is sent to us until the customer presses WhatsApp or email.

## 2.4 Downloading the PDF price list

In the search area, click **Download price list (PDF)**. You can download:
- the price list for the **brand you are viewing**, or
- the price list for **all brands**.

The PDF includes our logo, company name, website, contact details and page numbers.

# Part 3: Admin manual (step by step)

## 3.1 Signing in

1. Go to the website address followed by **/admin**. For example: `https://<our-website>/admin`.
   There is no admin link on the public website. This is deliberate, for security.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign in**.
3. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password?** and follow the email.

**Rules for admin accounts:**
- There is no "Sign up" page. Admin accounts are created only by the system owner (see section 3.13).
- You are **signed out automatically** after **30 minutes of no activity**, and when you close the browser.
- If someone's admin access is removed, they lose access within about 10 minutes, even if they are still signed in.

**The admin menu (left side):**

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Summary numbers and quick actions |
| **Products** | Find, edit, publish or hide products; change prices, offers and stock |
| **Brands & categories** | Brand names, order, and brand spotlight banners |
| **Import / export** | Update many products at once with Excel |
| **PDF price list** | Create a price list PDF |
| **Audit history** | Every price, offer and stock change |
| **Contacts** | Branches and salespeople on the Contact page |
| **Website settings** | Logo, texts, header, footer, layout, PDF design |
| **View public site ↗** | Opens the public website in a new tab |
| **Sign out** | Ends your session |

## 3.2 Dashboard

The dashboard shows counts:
- active products;
- in stock, low stock and out of stock;
- draft and archived products;
- products on offer (**Promotional products**);
- products **without a selling price**;
- products with **missing important information**.

Click a number to open the matching product list. Under **Quick actions** you can jump to common tasks.

## 3.3 Finding and editing a product

1. Click **Products**.
2. Type in the search box, or use the filters (brand, category, status, stock, offers, "Price-drop tag showing").
3. Click a product to open it.
4. Change what you need:
   - **Name, model, SKU (product code), brand, category**
   - **Description, specifications, key features**
   - **Price (SAR)** and **Offer price** with start and end dates
   - **Stock status** and quantity
   - **Picture** (upload) and **datasheet link**
   - **Visibility** (see the table below)
5. Click **Save**.

| Visibility | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Shown on the public website |
| **Draft** | Hidden. Use this for products still being prepared. |
| **Archived** | Hidden and set aside, for example discontinued products |

### Changing a price
- Open the product, type the new price and click **Save**. It appears on the website immediately.
- If the new price is **lower**, the website shows a green **PRICE ↓ %** tag for 30 days. The editor tells you this before you save.
- To remove a price-drop tag early, click **Remove tag** on the product page.
- Every price change is recorded in **Audit history**.

### Running an offer
- Enter an **Offer price**. It must be lower than the normal price.
- Choose the **start** and **end** dates, then save.
- The offer shows automatically between those dates and stops by itself afterwards.

### Changing many products at once (bulk actions)
In **Products**, tick the boxes next to several products. You can then:
- publish, unpublish or archive them;
- set their stock;
- clear their offers;
- export them to a spreadsheet.

### Adding a new product
Click **Products → Add product**, fill in the details and save. Set it to **Published** when it is ready.

## 3.4 Brands, categories and the brand spotlight

Go to **Brands & categories**. There you can:
- add or rename brands and categories. A rename updates every product automatically;
- **change the order** brands appear in on the website;
- delete brands or categories that no product uses.

**Brand spotlight** (the banner at the top of the website):
- For each brand you can set a **headline**, a **subtitle** and a **banner picture**.
- Banner pictures must be PNG, JPEG or WebP, at least 600 pixels wide and no bigger than 5 MB.
- When a visitor clicks that brand, the top banner changes to match.
- A new brand gets a spotlight automatically. Adding a headline and banner just makes it look better.

## 3.5 Updating many prices with Excel (Import / export)

Use this when many prices or stock levels change at once.

1. Go to **Import / export**.
2. **Step 1: Template.** Click **Download template (Excel)**, or use **Export catalog** to download all current products in the same format.
3. Edit the file in Excel. The main columns are:
   `sku, name, brand, model, category, subcategory, description, specifications, price_sar, offer_price_sar, stock_status, stock_quantity, image_url, source_url, datasheet_url, active`
4. **Step 2: Upload and preview.** Upload the file. The system shows a **preview** of exactly what will change, before anything is saved.
5. Check the preview, then confirm.

**Helpful rules:**
- Products are matched by **SKU** (product code).
- **Empty cells are left unchanged.** You only need to fill in what you want to change.
- Rows with problems, such as a duplicate SKU, are rejected and listed. The other rows still import.
- Every import appears in **Import history**. If something went wrong, click **Revert** on that import to undo it completely.

## 3.6 Creating a PDF price list

1. Go to **PDF price list**.
2. Choose:
   - a **title**;
   - whether to **include availability** (stock);
   - whether to **include key features** (up to 4 lines);
   - **which brands and categories**. Leave everything unticked to include all.
3. Click **Generate PDF**. The file downloads.

The logo, colours, footer text and contact details on the PDF are set in **Website settings → PDF branding**.

## 3.7 Audit history

**Audit history** lists every change to price, offer, offer dates, stock status and quantity:
- who made it;
- when;
- the old value and the new value.

This history **cannot be edited or deleted** by anyone, including admins. Changes to website settings and contacts are recorded too, in **Website settings → Change history**.

## 3.8 Managing contacts

Go to **Contacts**.
- **Branches:** add, rename, add a location, show or hide, change the order, or delete.
- **People:** add several people to a branch, each with a name, role (optional), phone and email.
- Phone numbers and emails are checked as you type. You cannot publish a wrong format.
- Click **Save and publish** to update the Contact page and the quote list branch choices. Click **Cancel** to throw away your changes.

> The **branches and people here are also the choices customers see on the Quote list page**. Keep them up to date.

## 3.9 Website settings

Go to **Website settings**. It has these tabs:

| Tab | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Company branding** | Company name, tagline, description, main website address, logo upload |
| **Header** | Which buttons show, their labels, menu links |
| **Footer** | Footer text, links, brand links, copyright, social media links |
| **Page content** | Home page title and text, catalog introduction, "About" section, stock display, **page layout** |
| **Contact information** | Main phone, WhatsApp number, email, address |
| **PDF branding** | Logo on/off, PDF title, tagline, contact and website on/off, footer text, sample PDF |
| **Change history** | Who changed what and when; you can reload an older version |
| **Security & access** | The list of admins and security advice |

**How saving works:**
- A preview of the header and footer updates as you type.
- Click **Save and publish** to make changes live. No programmer or redeploy is needed.
- If something is wrong, for example a website address without `https://`, the field turns red and publishing is blocked. The website keeps the last good version.
- **Restore defaults** loads the original settings into the editor. They do not go live until you click Save and publish.
- If you leave the page with unsaved changes, it warns you first.

### Choosing the page layout (Page content tab)

Under **Catalog layout on wide screens**:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | **Automatic**: big screens get the three-column wide layout. **Standard everywhere**: the classic centred layout on every screen. |
| **Use the wide layout on screens at least…** | 1,440 px (large laptops), **1,600 px (recommended)**, or 1,920 px (full-HD monitors) |
| **Let visitors switch between Wide and Standard** | When on, visitors with big screens get a small switch to choose for themselves |

Phones and tablets always use the standard layout.

# Part 4: Safety and security (plain-language summary)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can a visitor change prices? | **No.** Only signed-in admins can change anything. The database itself enforces this, not just the website. |
| Can someone make themselves an admin? | **No.** There is no sign-up page. Admin rights are given only by the system owner. |
| Can prices change automatically, or by an outside company? | **No.** There is no automatic price feed and no third-party access. Prices change only when an admin saves them, or imports a spreadsheet that the admin reviews first. |
| Can someone change a price secretly? | **No.** Every price, offer and stock change is recorded with the person's name and the time, and the record cannot be deleted. |
| Can a price-drop tag be faked? | **No.** The system only shows a tag when the price was really lowered. |
| Are supplier (source) prices shown to the public? | **No.** Reference prices are stored separately and only admins can see them. The website and PDFs show only Wider Universal prices. |
| What if someone leaves a computer signed in? | They are signed out after 30 minutes of no activity, and when the browser closes. |
| Is the website secure (https)? | Yes. Firebase Hosting always uses **https** (the padlock), including with our own domain. |
| What if a wrong setting is entered? | It is blocked before publishing. A damaged setting falls back to safe defaults, so the website never breaks. |

**Good habits for admins:**
- Use a strong password that you use only for this website.
- Never share your account. Each person should have their own.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Check **Audit history** from time to time.

# Part 7: Questions and problems

**I changed a price but the website still shows the old one.**
Refresh the page (press F5, or pull down on a phone). Changes are live immediately, but an open page does not reload itself.

**A product shows "Price on request".**
It has no price yet. Open it in **Products** and enter a price.

**A product is missing from the website.**
Check its **Visibility**. It must be **Published**. Also check that it isn't filtered out by a brand, category or "In stock" filter on the website.

**I was signed out.**
This happens after 30 minutes of no activity, or when the browser closes. Sign in again; saved work is not lost.

**"Save and publish" is greyed out or shows red fields.**
A field has a problem, such as a wrong email format or a missing `https://`. Fix the red fields and try again.

**An Excel import went wrong.**
Go to **Import / export → Import history** and click **Revert** on that import.

**A customer says the quote list disappeared.**
The list is stored only in the customer's own browser. It is lost if they clear their browser data, or if they use a different phone or computer.

**I can't see the Layout switch on the website.**
It appears only when Layout is **Automatic**, the visitor switch is turned on, and the screen is wide enough.
